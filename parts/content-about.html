About TechBait

A studio of engineers and designers who ship

Founded in 2018, TechBait partners with startups and growth-stage companies to design, build and scale the software behind their business.

Our story

From a two-person team to a full product studio

TechBait started with a simple frustration: too many agencies handed off pretty mockups that engineering teams struggled to build. We set out to do both — design and development — under one roof, with one team accountable for the outcome.

Eight years on, we've grown into a 45-person studio working with clients across fintech, healthtech and logistics, without losing the habit of showing up to every sprint demo ourselves.
