Our Process

A predictable path from idea to launch

No black-box sprints. Every stage ends with something you can see, click through, and give feedback on.

01
Week 1–2 · Discover

Discovery & scoping

We run stakeholder interviews, review existing data and competitors, and translate business goals into a build-ready product spec with clear success metrics.

02
Week 2–3 · Design

UX & UI design

Wireframes move to high-fidelity, interactive prototypes. We test flows with real users before any engineering time is spent.

03
Week 3 onward · Build

Engineering sprints

Two-week sprints with a public progress board, staging deploys on every merge, and a demo at the end of each sprint.

04
Pre-launch · QA

Testing & hardening

Automated test suites, manual QA passes, load testing and a security review before anything touches production.

05
Launch day

Deployment

We handle the release, monitor error rates and performance in real time, and stay on call through launch day.

06
Ongoing

Support & iteration

Post-launch, we move to a lighter support cadence — bug fixes, monitoring, and planning the next release together.
